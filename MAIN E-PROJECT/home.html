Our story

Baking like family is watching.

"Where Smiles Are Served Daily."

Bakerz Bite started with one rule: never put anything on the counter we wouldn't happily serve our own family. That means real butter instead of substitutes, real cream instead of stabilisers, and unbleached flour milled the way flour is supposed to be. It's a slower, more expensive way to bake — and it's the only way we do it.
